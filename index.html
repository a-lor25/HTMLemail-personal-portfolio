    About Me

    I am a disciplined, self-taught Email Developer who specializes in turning design concepts into pixel-perfect, responsive HTML and MJML code that renders beautifully in any inbox. Based out of Sun Prairie, WI, I build clean, high-performance email assets ready for production and remote deployment.
